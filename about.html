About CSVM.

Christ Salvation Victory Mission International is a place of encounter with the Almighty God, committed to holiness of life, daily victory and evangelism.

Who we are

A ministry centred on
the Word and prayer.

His saving grace and abundant power to deliver and set free from sin and its consequences are powerfully available. At CSVM, we believe in prevailing prayers and a deep study of God’s Word.

Holiness of life is our watchword, daily victory is our life experience, and evangelism is our supreme task. You are welcome to CSVM… Where God is doing it!

Our vision

Preaching Christ
to all nations.

Our vision is to preach the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ throughout all nations, beginning from Jerusalem, our immediate locality. We have been given the mandate to produce a holy, royal priesthood that stands out wherever our converts find themselves.

Our mission

To decongest hell
and populate heaven.

We seek to bring sinners to the kingdom of light and win back the backslidden through the pursuit of holiness, prayer and the ministry of the Word.

We are committed to raising Christian leaders through integrity, tenacity of purpose and righteous living, and to planting vibrant churches where people of all races, colours and languages worship God in Spirit, love and truth.

Our core values

Lives shaped by
the fruit of the Spirit.

Our core values are expressed through the manifestation of the fruits of the Spirit, as outlined in Galatians 5:22, by every member of the CSVM family worldwide.

Our heritage

A story that began
in 1984.

CSVM was founded in response to a divine mandate given to Reverend Samuel Ajeje Falodun to pioneer a soul-winning ministry built on personal evangelism, the Word of God and prayer.

1981

A new chapter of calling

Reverend Samuel Ajeje Falodun took a dignified exit from the Redeemed Christian Church of God and later worked with His Coming Evangelical Church Inc. in Ibadan.

1984

CSVM is founded

The assignment became clear. Ministry outreach began in Orile and Sari Iganmu, as well as Badia, Ijora and Amukoko in Lagos, sharing messages centred on the gospel.

1990

Leadership transition

Reverend Samuel Ajeje Falodun passed away on 12 October 1990. Elder Victor Olufemi Fasehun took over leadership of the young ministry.

1993

A further handover

On 31 October 1993, Elder Victor Olufemi Fasehun handed over leadership to Pastor Emmanuel Olu Falodun.

1998

Ordination

Pastor Emmanuel Oluwemimo Falodun was ordained as Senior Pastor on 9 November 1998 by Pastor E. A. Adeboye, General Overseer of the Redeemed Christian Church of God.

2010

Victory Camp first service

The first service at Victory Camp was held on Sunday, 31 October 2010. The first preacher there was Evangelist Sylvia Suhr from Australia.

The dates and historical details above follow the information supplied by the church.

Faith in action

Community impact.

Alongside its evangelistic work, CSVM describes community support as part of its commitment to reaching people with the gospel.

01

Annual All Believers Fasting and Prayer

This 21-day programme dates back to 1994 and takes place between mid-October and the first Sunday of November each year. The church describes 100 days of chain prayers and fasting as preparation before the public programme.

02

Health and welfare outreach

The church has provided free health checks and medical services to rural communities in Ogun State and donated food and clothing items.

03

Ajerogun electrification project

CSVM reports assisting with an application for support to the Ogun State Government and following up until the supply of a 500KVA transformer to the State Government was achieved.
